Tunetastic — pane / settings / back button icons

Use the tabs to check the four color treatments · click the title-bar buttons and the sidebar rows — the pane toggle actually collapses the pane and its chevron flips direction. Hues for the new three are placeholders pending your call. Nothing has been changed in the app.
